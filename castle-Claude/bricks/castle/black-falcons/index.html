Black Falcons

House of Black Falcon2021–2025 · 2002–2003 · 1984–199021 sets32 minifigures

The second big faction of 1984. LEGO.com introduces it as what fans believed was a more sinister faction, led by the mighty Black Knight – the perfect rival for ongoing skirmishes with the Lion Knights. Fans think they disappeared for a while after the Lion Knights' attack in 6062 Battering Ram (1987); they returned in LEGO Ideas 21325 Medieval Blacksmith and have appeared in sets ever since. Regional names: Eagle Crest (Australia, Canada); the 10305 instructions say “House of Black Falcon”.

Allies and enemies

  • Rivals of the Lion Knights – LEGO.com speaks of ongoing skirmishes. official
  • According to fan legend (LEGO.com), their leader fled to his ancestral home after defeat and founded the Black Knights. fan reading
  • Besiege the Black Knights in 6059; the Black Knights sail against them in 6057. official
  • 2022: the 10305 box – the rivalry with the Lion Knights ends and the falcons arrive as friends. official

  17. 2021An exclusive LEGO Ideas set based on a design by Clemens Fiedler who, according to LEGO, learned blacksmithing as a child in Germany and brought it into the details of the model. Three floors that lift apart: a fully equipped forge whose fire lights up when you press the bellows, a kitchen above and a bedroom in the attic.   18. 2021A Creator 3in1 castle (Jonathan Robson, Morten Rauff): a gate, a smithy, a water mill, a prison and the king's room, with an opening back. According to LEGO it rebuilds into a castle tower or a marketplace; three copies combine into one big castle.
  19. 2021A castle in the forest from the first BrickLink Designer Program round (2021): a cave, a raft, a trapdoor, a portcullis, target practice, a kitchen and a bedroom. Six figures – Forestmen and Black Falcons.
  20. 2022The flagship of the classics' return. It was made for LEGO's 90th anniversary at fans' request: the theme was chosen in a LEGO Ideas poll in which, according to LEGO, 55,000 people took part. It is a new version of the 1984 castle 6080 (designers Milan Madge, Mike Psiaki and Carl Merriam), sold only at LEGO.com and LEGO Stores. Closed, it measures 38 × 44 × 33 cm; its two halves open to almost 70 cm. Inside: a stable, an armoury, a dungeon, a kitchen with a mill and a working water wheel, a dining hall and the queen's chamber with a miniature 375 castle. The drawbridge and portcullis can be raised and lowered, moving walls hide secret passages, and beneath the castle is the forest folk's hideout – when they attack, a trapdoor drops them straight into the dungeon, from which they can escape through a secret tunnel. 22 minifigures: the queen and Lion Knights, Black Falcons, Forestmen and the wizard Majisto. The box copies the 1980s LEGOLAND Castle style.

  23. 2025A forest stronghold (BrickLink Designer Program Series 3) in two halves joined by a bridge and a zip line between the towers.
