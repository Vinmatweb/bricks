31168 · Creator 3in1

Medieval Horse Knight Castle

2025 · 1,371 pieces · 6 minifigures ● on sale

A Creator 3in1 castle by designer Jonathan Robson that introduced the new Horse Knights faction. A working drawbridge, a portcullis, a stone watchtower, Tudor-style living quarters with a blue roof, a great hall with a throne and chandelier, the king's bedroom, a fireplace that doubles as a forge, a stable and a hidden treasure spot under the stairs. Six minifigures – Horse Knights including the king and a champion, plus one Serpent Knight as a rival – and two horses. According to LEGO the castle rebuilds into a jousting tournament or a medieval town with a windmill.

21358 · Ideas

Minifigure Vending Machine

2025 · 1,343 pieces · 16 minifigures ● on sale

LEGO Ideas No. 66, based on a fan design. A fully working vending machine: insert a coin, turn the handle and a capsule with a figure drops out. According to LEGO, the sixteen collectible figures pay tribute to classic themes such as Castle, Paradisa and Fabuland; four of them were chosen by LEGO Ideas fans – among them the new castle factions Griffin Knights and Kraken Warriors.
