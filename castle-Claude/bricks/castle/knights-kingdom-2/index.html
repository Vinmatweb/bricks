Knights' Kingdom II

The knights of Morcia2004–200628 sets38 minifigures

A relaunch with colourful knights of the kingdom of Morcia, each with a name and personality, battling the evil knight Vladek. LEGO History calls it a radical departure from previous castle lines – more action-oriented, with near-superhero abilities. Buildable action figures and minifigures, a trading card game, books and a Game Boy Advance game.

Crest and colours

Each knight has a colour and an animal: Jayko – light blue, hawk; Santis – red, bear; Rascus – green, monkey; Danju – purple, wolf; King Mathias – gold, lion. Vladek – black, scorpion.

Characters
  • King Mathias, later King Jayko
  • Knights Jayko, Santis, Rascus, Danju; from 2006 Sir Kentis and Sir Adric
  • Vladek (Lord Vladek) and the Shadow Knights; in 2006 the Rogue Knights Dracus and Karzon

History

  • 2006: King Jayko, Adric and Kentis race the Rogue Knights to the Mistlands Tower; Vladek is sealed in a crystal.
  • 2005: Vladek flees to the lost kingdom of Ankoria and builds the Vladmask; Jayko destroys it.
  • 2004: Vladek, King Mathias's adviser, imprisons the king and uses the Book of Morcia to turn knights into Shadow Knights. The four heroes free the king.
