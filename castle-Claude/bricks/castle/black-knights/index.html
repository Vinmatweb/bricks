Classic era (1978–1999)

Black Knights

Dragon crest knights2026 · 1988–199418 sets32 minifigures

According to the fan legend told on LEGO.com, after the Black Falcons' defeat the Black Knight escaped to his ancestral home and founded the Black Knights. They first appeared in 6085 Black Monarch's Castle (1988) and continued in sets like 6057 Sea Serpent and 6086 Black Knight's Castle.

Crest and colours

Unmarked armour; only the blue dragon on their shields and horse barding identifies them (LEGO.com). Black castles.

Characters
  • No names; “Black Monarch” appears only in set titles.

Sets with this faction

40912 · Gift with purchase (Ideas)

Sea Serpent

2026 · 241 pieces · 2 minifigures ○ retired

A new version of the 1992 ship 6057 that won the LEGO Ideas design contest “A Twist of Nostalgia” (design by HallowedBrick02, finished by Mel Caddick). A fabric sail, a modular stern, movable rudders, flags and a cargo hold. The crew are two knights that the Rebrickable catalogue lists as Griffin Knights in a dark blue variant. 241 pieces, about 20 cm long. A gift with purchase during Insiders Days in July 2026.

Black Knight's Castle

1992 · 590 pieces ○ retired

The Black Knights' black castle on a raised baseplate: a drawbridge, a portcullis, a dungeon with a Wolfpack prisoner and a well. 588 pieces, twelve figures. Designed by Niels Milan Pedersen.

eBayBrickset

Minifigures 32 variants

All catalogued figure variants of the faction with the sets they come in (Rebrickable inventories, 29 Oct 2026). Faction assignment is editorial, not an official LEGO taxonomy.

Allies and enemies

  • Successors of the Black Falcons according to the legend on LEGO.com. fan reading
  • Hunt the Wolfpack – a prisoner in 6086, an attack in 6057. official
  • From 1993 they fear the Dragon Masters' dragons (LEGO.com: their roars sent even the bravest knights running). official
  • Capture Robin Hood (Forestmen) in a LEGO commercial. official
  • Fight the Lion Knights in the 1992 UK adverts. official

History

  • 2026: gift 40912 Sea Serpent, a new version of 6057 based on the winning entry of the LEGO Ideas contest “A Twist of Nostalgia”.
  • 1992: 6086 Black Knight's Castle and 6057 Sea Serpent.
  • 1988: 6085 Black Monarch's Castle.
