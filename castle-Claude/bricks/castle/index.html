Fan guide

What is LEGO Castle

LEGO Castle is one of the oldest LEGO themes: knights, castles, siege engines and later wizards and dragons. It was born in 1978, when owner Kjeld Kirk Kristiansen introduced play themes as we know them today; one of the first was LEGOLAND Castle, whose only set was the yellow castle 375. It gradually split into factions with their own crests, colours and enemies. The standalone line ended in 2014, but since 2021 the classic factions have been returning in LEGO Icons, Ideas, Creator, the BrickLink Designer Program and gifts with purchase.

Beginnings

1978 – the first modern minifigures came out in Castle, Space and Town (designer Jens Nygaard Knudsen). The first knights had movable visors and brick-built horses.

Factions

From 1984 knights are divided by the symbols on their shields; according to LEGO History the biggest factions were the Crusaders (Lion Knights) and the Black Falcons. This site follows 21 groups.

Eras of the Castle line

  • 1978–1983, the classic castle. The yellow castle 375, then 383 Knight's Tournament and 677 Knight's Procession (1979). Knights with movable visors and coats of arms on tunics or breastplates; horses built from bricks; no story.
  • 1984–1997, the golden age of factions. More realistic castles and new elements: one-piece horses, saddles, fixed-visor helmets, plumes, flags, bows and arrows. Lion Knights against Black Falcons; civilians such as peasants and blacksmiths in sets like 6041 Armor Shop and 6067 Guarded Inn (1986). Then the Forestmen (1987), the Black Knights (1988) and the Wolfpack (1992) – the year castle figures first got printed faces. In 1993 the Dragon Masters took the line into fantasy, in 1995 the first crowned king arrived with the Royal Knights, and in 1997 the Fright Knights closed the era.
  • 2000–2006, stories. Knights' Kingdom with the first full royal family (King Leo, Queen Leonora, Prince Edward and Princess Storm), the more action-oriented Knights' Kingdom II with named knights, Vikings.
  • 2007–2014, the Castle name returns. Castle 2007 with skeletons, trolls and dwarves, the realistic Kingdoms (Lion Kingdom against Dragon Kingdom), the 2013 line combining history and fantasy. In 2016 LEGO followed with NEXO Knights.
  • 2016–today, nostalgia. Reimagined classics for adult fans: 10305 Lion Knights' Castle, 10332 Medieval Town Square, the BrickLink Designer Program, remake gifts 40567, 40601 and 40912.
