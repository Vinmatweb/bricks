Fantasy & Kingdoms (2007–2014)

Castle: Fantasy Era

Crown Knights, skeletons, trolls and dwarves2024–2025 · 2007–200934 sets91 minifigures

The Castle name returned in 2007. According to LEGO History it goes back to a traditional medieval setting, repeats fantasy elements such as dragons and sorcerers and adds new ones: skeleton warriors, trolls and dwarves. A single human kingdom fights non-human enemies – an evil wizard's skeletons in 2007, trolls from 2008.

Crest and colours

Crown Knights: a crown; dark blue, light blue, gold. Skeletons: a skull; dark red and black. Dwarves: a king with a golden winged helmet.

Characters
  • King, Queen, Princess, the gold Champion knight, the court wizard, the jester
  • The evil wizard / necromancer, skeletons, the reaper
  • Troll King, Troll Sorceress, giant trolls
  • The Dwarf King

10332 · Icons

Medieval Town Square

2024 · 3,304 pieces · 8 minifigures ○ retired

According to LEGO, the village of Felsa, inspired by the 2009 set 10193 Medieval Market Village and a companion to castle 10305 (designers Henrik Rubin Saaby and Rok Žgalin Kobe). A square with an old tree where villagers trade goods, the tax collector's house with a secret money compartment, wanted posters, a shield painter's workshop finishing a Lion Knights shield, the Broken Axe tavern, a farmhouse with a tame goat, a stall with cheese, pretzels and pastries, and a watchtower where a Lion Knight keeps guard. Eight figures, including the return of the Wolfpack.

10193

Medieval Market Village

2009 · 1,619 pieces ○ retired

A medieval market village: two hinged two-storey buildings – a tavern and a smithy where a water wheel drives a hammer. A market stall, a tree, a horse-drawn hay cart and two cows. Eight figures with Crown Knights. The basis for 10332 in 2024.
